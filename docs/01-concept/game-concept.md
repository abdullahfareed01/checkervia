# Game Concept

- **Name:** Draft
- **Genre:** Turn based board game (checkers), browser
- **Players:** 2 on one screen, or 1 vs LLM AI
- **One line pitch:** Classic checkers with strict, fully tested rules and an AI opponent that must pick from verified legal moves.

## Core fantasy
- Outthink the opponent by planning captures and chains.

## Session
- One full game: roughly 5 to 15 minutes.
- Start, alternate turns, someone wins, press New game.

## Modes
- Two players (hot seat)
- Versus AI (human plays black by default, AI plays white)

## What makes it different
- The AI never chooses freely: the code lists legal moves and the model only picks one.
- Rules engine is pure and tested, so every rule can be explained and demonstrated.

## Out of scope
- Online multiplayer, accounts, ratings, draw rules.
